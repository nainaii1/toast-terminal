Method

How the bill is counted.

Trading spread

The exchange charges no fee, so the cost of a trade is the gap between its buy and sell quote. For every quick buy-then-sell I count buy price minus sell price, times size, from my own trade export.

Other costs

Everything else trading cost me that week, as one figure. A week can show a negative number here when those results were a gain.

Per point

Total divided by the points the exchange paid for that week. Points arrive on Friday for the week that closed on Thursday.

What is left out

What a point will be worth. There is no token yet, so this page is a cost record, not a result, and not advice.
